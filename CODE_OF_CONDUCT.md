# Code of Conduct

Be civil. Do not harass people. Do not dump private data, secrets, or
exploit details into issues.

This is a small editorial-skill project. Disagreements about taste
(Pass H slips, stance, roughness) are product arguments, not personal
ones. Argue the text.

Maintainers may close issues or PRs that ignore `SECURITY.md` or
`CONTRIBUTING.md`.
